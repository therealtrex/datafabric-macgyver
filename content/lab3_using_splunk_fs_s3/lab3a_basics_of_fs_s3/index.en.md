## Overview

For lesson one we will take a look at the basics of how Splunk's Federated Search for Amazon S3 (FS-S3) works. As mentioned earlier this first lesson will take a look at CloudFront access logs as our first use case.

After this lesson you will start to understand the following concepts of FS-S3:

1. **CloudFront Access Log Setup**: Quick look at how CloudFront Access Logs are set up for Amazon S3.

2. **Familiarize yourself with Amazon Glue**: Understand Amazon Glue and how it is used in the Splunk's Federated Search for Amazon S3 product.

3. **Create a federated dataset**: Using our first data source (CloudFront Access Logs) you will configure your first federated dataset, setup required permissions in AWS and look at a basic search result from FS-S3.
